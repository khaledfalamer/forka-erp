Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Forka Apps (erp.forka.com) is the internal work portal of Forka Collective Investment LLC and its brands (including Al Matbakh Al Kuwaiti). It is used only by our employees and people we authorise.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive only your name and email address, to check whether you are allowed to use the portal. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Staff who sign in with a PIN have their PIN stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form.

How we use it

To give you access to the work apps you are authorised for, to handle access requests, and to keep a record of activity in the apps (for example: who signed in, which app was opened, who changed access). This record is visible only to company management.

Where it is kept

In the company's private Google Workspace account. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone outside the company, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long

For as long as you work with us or need access. When access ends, your account is blocked and can be deleted on request.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or stop using Google sign-in, by emailing ceo@forka.com.

Contact

Forka Collective Investment LLC - ceo@forka.com

تحفظ البيانات في حساب Google Workspace الخاص بالشركة، ولا نبيعها ولا نشاركها مع أي جهة خارج الشركة ولا نستخدمها للإعلانات.
