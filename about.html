Who can use it

Only employees of Forka Collective Investment LLC and its brands (including Al Matbakh Al Kuwaiti), and people the company authorises. New people can request access after signing in with Google; management approves or rejects each request and decides which apps each person can open.

How Google sign-in is used

We use Google sign-in only to confirm your name and email address. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. See our Privacy Policy.
